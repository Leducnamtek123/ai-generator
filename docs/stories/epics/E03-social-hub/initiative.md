# Social Hub Facebook-First Multichannel Initiative

## Goal

Turn the Social Hub into a Facebook-first reference product that still scales to
other social channels through one shared workspace model.

## Why This Initiative Exists

The current contract covers dashboard analytics, channel connection, inbox
interactions, and early calendar direction, but the product intent is broader:

- Facebook should be the canonical baseline for learning and UX decisions.
- The product should behave like a pipeline, not just a post composer.
- The roadmap needs a durable structure for publishing, inbox, listening,
  analytics, governance, and content reuse.

## Product References

- Sprout Social for overall workflow structure.
- Meta Business Suite for Facebook-native behavior.
- Hootsuite for multichannel inbox and automation patterns.
- Buffer for simple scheduling and community workflows.
- Later for calendar and approval operations.

## Product Slices

### Foundation

- Connect Facebook Pages through OAuth.
- Review returned pages and select which pages become publish targets.
- Support reconnect, disconnect, token refresh, and multi-page workspace
  management.

### Publishing

- Draft posts for text, image, video, link, and multi-photo content.
- Support scheduling, rescheduling, duplication, deletion, and platform
  customization.
- Keep approval flows and queue/slot logic in the product shape.

### Inbox and Interaction

- Handle comments and messages in one inbox.
- Support assign, mark handled, saved replies, AI suggestions, notes, labels,
  and moderation actions where allowed.

### Monitoring and Listening

- Track mentions, keywords, brand activity, spikes, and conversation history.

### Analytics and Governance

- Measure reach, impressions, engagement, growth, and exports.
- Add role-based access, approval chains, audit logs, and response tracking.

### Content Sources and Reuse

- Support media libraries, brand assets, templates, RSS/URL ingest, imported
  content, and approved content reuse.

## Exit Criteria

- The backlog is split into slices that map cleanly to the product pipeline.
- Future stories can be added without rewriting the Social Hub contract.
- Facebook-first assumptions are explicit in the product docs.
